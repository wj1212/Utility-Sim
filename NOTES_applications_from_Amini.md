## Domains he listed

Autonomous vehicles · military · gaming · home appliances · **healthcare**

## Healthcare examples

**Fall-prevention wearable**
Gait instability, fatigue, obstacle risk → stay quiet / vibration warning / recommend rest.
He specifically noted: *"The inertia component could prevent annoying repeated alerts
while still responding quickly when fall risk increases."*
→ Closest analogue to our setup.

**Chronic-disease management (diabetes assistant)**
Glucose, hypoglycemia risk, exercise, food intake → monitor / notify / escalate.

**Rehabilitation system**
Performance, fatigue, movement quality, discomfort → continue / change difficulty /
assist / suggest a break.

**Hospital delivery robot**
Battery, delivery urgency, distance, patient priority → continue / recharge /
interrupt a routine delivery for an urgent one.

**Patient monitoring**
Distinguish temporary vital-sign changes from sustained deterioration.
Reduces false alarms without slowing real response.

**Intelligent walker**
Adjusts assistance level from gait instability, fatigue, obstacles, fall risk —
*"without constantly switching back and forth."*
